Oxford Park

A scenic public park where families, walkers, and art lovers can enjoy a relaxing day outdoors.

Park Introduction

Oxford Park is a peaceful destination filled with open lawns, shaded walkways, and beautiful public art. It is a favorite place for community gatherings, weekend strolls, and sunny afternoons with friends and family.

Things to Do

  • Take a relaxing walk along the winding paths
  • Enjoy a picnic in the grassy open areas
  • Visit the outdoor art displays and installations
  • Watch local performances and community events
  • Spend time with family at the playground and seating areas

Plan Your Visit

Bring comfortable walking shoes, pack water, and check the park's event calendar for any special programs or exhibitions. The park is most enjoyable in the morning or late afternoon when the weather is cooler and the paths are quieter.

For more information about nearby art exhibits and cultural events, visit the Ashmolean Museum.
